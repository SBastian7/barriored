# 04 — Brand, Voice & Content Guidelines

This file lets the project produce on-brand content (posts, flyers, scripts, copy) without re-explaining the brand each time.

## Brand essence

**Neo-Brutalist Tropical** — bold, honest, and warm. The visual language is unapologetic and structural (thick black borders, hard shadows, uppercase), softened by tropical Latin American warmth (vibrant reds, sun yellows, street-art blues). It should feel like the neighborhood itself: direct, vivid, proud, human.

- **Personality:** confident, warm, hopeful, plainspoken, a little playful.
- **Not:** corporate, sterile, paternalistic, charity-flavored, or "techy."

## Voice & tone

- **Spanish (Colombia), informal "tú".** Talk like a trusted neighbor, not a bank or an NGO.
- **Warm and direct.** Short sentences. Real words. No jargon.
- **Empowering, never pitying.** Merchants and residents are protagonists. Avoid "ayudar a los más necesitados" framing; prefer "tu negocio", "tu barrio", "lo nuestro".
- **Action-oriented.** Lead with what the person gains and what to do next.
- **Proud of the local.** Celebrate the barrio, its people, its commerce.

### Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Pon tu negocio en el mapa del barrio." | "Digitaliza tu emprendimiento con nuestra plataforma." |
| "Sin papeles, sin enredos, gratis." | "Regístrate cumpliendo los requisitos." |
| "Todo tu barrio en un solo lugar." | "Una solución integral para la comunidad." |
| "Escríbele directo por WhatsApp." | "Contacta al proveedor del servicio." |
| Warm, concrete, local | Corporate, abstract, generic |

### Sample voice snippets (reuse / adapt)

- **Merchant CTA:** "¿Tienes un negocio en el barrio? Ponlo en BarrioRed gratis. Fotos, horario, ubicación y botón directo a tu WhatsApp. Sin papeles."
- **Resident CTA:** "Encuentra lo que necesitas cerquita: negocios, anuncios, alertas y lo que se vende en tu barrio. Todo en un solo lugar."
- **Alert tone:** "🚱 Corte de agua mañana en el sector. Guarda agua y avísale a tus vecinos."
- **Community pride:** "El barrio se mueve. Apoya lo local, compra cerca, conoce a tu gente."

## Naming & key terms

- **BarrioRed** — the platform (always one word, capital B and R).
- **Barrio / vecino / vecina** — neighborhood / neighbor; use warmly and often.
- The four pillars, by their Spanish names: **Directorio**, **Comunidad** (a.k.a. **Red Vecinal**), **Marketplace** (a.k.a. **Clasificados**), **Servicios**.
- Avoid English product words in user-facing Spanish copy where a natural Spanish word exists.

## Visual identity (for briefs, flyers, social templates)

> You won't write CSS here, but you'll brief designers and generate layout/content ideas. Keep these rules.

### Style rules
- **Borders:** 2–4px solid **black**, always present.
- **Shadows:** hard offset shadows (e.g. a 4px black drop, no blur). No soft/gradient shadows.
- **Corners:** mostly square; minimal rounding.
- **Badges:** slightly rotated (`-2°`) for a playful, hand-placed feel.
- **Labels/nav:** UPPERCASE, wide letter-spacing.
- **Layout:** bold blocks, thick dividers, stat strips with heavy borders, generous contrast.

### Typography
- **Headings:** *Outfit* — black weight, uppercase, tight tracking, often italic. Loud and confident.
- **Body:** *Inter* — clean, highly legible.

### Color palette
| Role | Name | Feel | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Barrio Red** | signature, energetic | main brand, primary buttons, hero |
| Secondary | **Sun Yellow** | warm, sunny | accents, badges, highlights |
| Accent | **Street Art Blue** | informational | links, info elements |
| Background | **Warm paper white** | warm neutral | backgrounds |
| Foreground | **Deep navy black** | grounding | text |
| Borders | **Pure black** | brutalist structure | all borders |
| Support | **Jungle Green**, **Terracotta** | tropical | charts, secondary accents |

(Exact oklch values live in the engineering workspace; for content/design briefs, the names and feel above are enough.)

### Logo / wordmark usage
- "BarrioRed" set in the bold heading style; high contrast; on warm-white or red blocks.
- Keep it loud, square, and unmistakable. Never thin, pastel, or corporate.

## Content patterns you'll be asked to produce

- **Social posts** (Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp states/TikTok captions) — merchant spotlights, "negocio de la semana", alerts, events, marketplace finds, neighborhood pride.
- **Merchant onboarding** — scripts (door-to-door, WhatsApp), one-pagers, FAQ, "cómo registrarte en 3 pasos".
- **Resident acquisition** — flyers, QR posters, WhatsApp-group messages, launch announcements.
- **Community partnerships** — outreach to JAC, líderes, fundaciones, comuna.
- **Funding/impact** — grant narratives, investor one-pagers, social-impact summaries (label all metrics as targets unless told otherwise).
- **In-app copy** — Spanish microcopy for screens, empty states, notifications.

### Defaults when producing content
- Mobile-first formatting; short lines; emoji used sparingly and purposefully (alerts, energy), never to look "salesy".
- Always include a clear next step (register, share, contact, attend).
- For merchant-facing content, lead with **free + no paperwork + WhatsApp**.
- For resident-facing content, lead with **everything-local-in-one-place + trust**.
