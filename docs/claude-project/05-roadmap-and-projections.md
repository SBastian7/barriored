## Go-to-market motion (recommended)

1. **Seed supply (merchants) first.** A directory with 50–100 real businesses is the magnet. Door-to-door + WhatsApp onboarding, free, no paperwork.
2. **Activate community leaders.** JAC, líderes, fundaciones as trusted multipliers.
3. **Drive demand (residents).** QR posters in seeded businesses, WhatsApp groups, local events, "negocio de la semana" content.
4. **Switch on the social layer.** Alerts and events give residents a reason to return weekly, not just once.
5. **Introduce monetization gently** only after adoption — featured businesses and banners, framed as "destácate en tu barrio," not as a paywall.

## Monetization model

- **Free core forever** — listing, browsing, community, marketplace, services. Access is the mission.
- **Premium / featured businesses** — paid highlight in listings + analytics. Primary revenue.
- **Banner ads** on the homepage — local sponsors and businesses.
- **Featured marketplace listings** — 🔜 paid boost for classifieds.
- **Payments:** start **manual / Nequi-style transfer**, then add gateways (Wompi / MercadoPago) as volume grows.
- **Future possibilities:** institutional/sponsor partnerships, comuna or municipal collaborations, value-added services for merchants.

**Pricing principle:** small absolute prices in COP, aligned with what a barrio merchant can actually pay; monetization must never gate basic visibility.

## Replication / expansion strategy

BarrioRed is **multi-tenant by design** — new neighborhoods are new instances (`barriored.co/{neighborhood}`), not new builds.

- **Prove the pilot** in Parque Industrial: adoption, retention, first revenue, social impact.
- **Package the playbook:** seeding, onboarding, partnerships, content — so a new barrio launches from a template.
- **Expand within Pereira / Comuna del Café first** (candidates referenced in planning: e.g. Cuba, Villa Santana), then other cities.
- **Scale the network** of neighborhood platforms across Colombia's popular economy.

## Social impact (measure and report — as goals until real)

Track and tell the story around:
- Number of **informal businesses getting their first digital presence**.
- **Reported sales increase** for participating merchants.
- **Neighbor connections** facilitated (events attended, posts, marketplace exchanges).
- **Local jobs** connected through the platform.
- Community resilience: alerts delivered, services surfaced, participation in the board.

This impact narrative is core to grant and partnership materials — keep it concrete, local, and human, and always distinguish **target** from **measured**.

## How this project should use the roadmap

- Ground plans in the **real state**: product is ready; the bottleneck is **adoption, trust, and seeding**, not features.
- When asked for strategy, bias toward **getting the first real merchants and residents**, then retention, then revenue.
- When asked for funding/impact materials, use these projections as clearly-labeled goals and lean on the differentiators in file 01.
